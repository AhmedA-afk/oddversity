Two things worth noticing before you round any of this off in your head. Anthropic's floor is the highest of anything posted, at roughly double Palantir's and Vercel's floors, which sit at the bottom of the range. And every one of these is a base range: equity, sign-on, and RSU vesting schedules sit outside the number and change the real total by a large margin, especially at Palantir, where the base looks modest against the total-comp figures below.

## What the aggregators report

Two aggregator pages were fetched directly, both current as of the date this path was written.

- **Levels.fyi, Forward Deployed Engineer (US, cross-company):** median total comp $206,073; 25th percentile $175,000; 75th percentile $278,000; 90th percentile $350,000.
- **Levels.fyi, Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer specifically:** range $185,000–$631,250; median shown as roughly $257,000–$278,000 depending on which figure on the page you read; an example package at the SWE level: $227,000 total ($164,000 base, $57,400 stock per year, $5,200 bonus).

One further figure exists only as a synthesis, not a fetched posting: Perspective AI's compensation report aggregates Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, Blind and pay-transparency postings and states frontier-lab mid-level pay in the $385,000–$510,000 band, senior at $560,000–$785,000. Treat this the way you would treat any vendor survey in this path: directional, not a number to quote as fact, because its methodology is a synthesis of secondary sources rather than a set of postings anyone can check.

## No India posting states comp. State this plainly.

Every India-located or India-remote posting checked for this path — Databricks Remote-India, Sarvam AI (Bengaluru), Razorpay (Bengaluru), HackerRank (Bengaluru, since closed), Peakflo, SigNoz — was silent on compensation. Peakflo is the one partial exception: its own YC listing states ₹10–13 lakh. No other verified India posting states a number at all.

Where a number circulates for the Indian market, it comes from a secondary source, not a posting: fde.academy's own guide cites salary bands from roughly ₹18–28 lakh at junior startup level up to ₹90 lakh–₹1.5 crore at senior global-lab level, and separately notes that "international bands run 50–70% of US" is a line from one vendor, not a documented figure. Use these as a starting orientation for what to expect, not as a number to repeat in a negotiation, because neither is sourced to an actual offer.

## Travel, stated plainly by the postings themselves

Across the postings surveyed for this path, travel is stated explicitly in eleven of them, clustering between 20% and 50% of working time. Anthropic states roughly 25%. OpenAI states travel "up to 50%." Even the one verified India-remote posting, Databricks, states travel to customer sites "once every 4 to 8 weeks" despite being fully remote day to day. Read "remote" on an FDE posting as "remote between trips," not as "no travel."

## The experience floor

This is the wall that matters most for anyone starting this path from zero.

At the labs and larger platforms, the floor is four to five years or more: Anthropic asks for four-plus years of customer-facing technical work, or an equivalent founder or consulting background; OpenAI's Forward Deployed Software Engineer track goes as high as seven years stated. This is not a floor a nine-month path closes on its own.

The India-verified postings sit lower, and this is the realistic target. Sarvam AI's Bengaluru posting asks for 2 to 5 years. Razorpay's Bengaluru posting asks for 4-plus years and daily fluency with AI coding tools. Peakflo's India-remote posting is the lowest floor found anywhere in this research, at 0.5 to 2 years, though it is also the only one with a stated (and modest) salary, which tells you something about where on the market it sits.

## What this means if you are starting from zero

Do not target a lab FDE role as your first seat. The floor is not reachable in nine months from zero, and every source that discusses timelines agrees: vendor estimates for a solutions-engineer-to-FDE transition run 6 to 12 months, and 18 to 24 months from a non-technical starting point. This path is built to get you past the 2-to-5-year floor that Sarvam, Razorpay-adjacent startups, and India-remote roles like Peakflo actually post, not past Anthropic's four-year floor in nine months. Phase 08 covers exactly which seats to target first and how to negotiate once you have an offer in hand; this page exists so you walk into that phase with real numbers instead of the round ones that circulate on social media.
