There is a lot written about Forward Deployed Engineering in India and very little of it can be checked. This page keeps two lists apart on purpose: postings that were actually opened and read, and claims that appear only on sites whose business is selling courses, placement or recruiting to the people reading them.

You should hold the second list at arm's length. Not because it is necessarily false, but because you would be planning nine months of your life around it.

## Part one: postings that were verified

Each of these was fetched, not summarised from a search result.

**Databricks, AI Engineer - FDE, Remote India.** Filed under professional services. Requires generative-AI application experience covering retrieval-augmented generation, multi-agent systems, text-to-SQL and fine-tuning; production GenAI deployment with evaluation and optimisation; substantial hands-on data-science experience with pandas, scikit-learn and PyTorch; production machine learning on AWS, Azure or GCP; a graduate degree in a quantitative discipline or equivalent practical experience. Travel "once every 4-8 weeks to see customers". No compensation stated.

**Sarvam AI, Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Bengaluru.** Two to five years of software engineering with a track record of shipping production systems. Strong Python across the stack. A "deep understanding of how LLMs and agents work: context windows, tokens, embeddings, RAG, tool calling, prompt design, and failure modes". Multi-agent architectures with memory and orchestration. Evaluation pipelines and quality metrics. The posting describes four tracks, including conversational agents over voice, WhatsApp and web, and a backend track naming MCP servers, LangGraph and Kafka or SQS or RabbitMQ pipelines. Its phrasing on ownership: "High agency: you unblock yourself, figure things out, and ship without hand-holding." It also says "ship real solutions fast, not prototypes or demos". No compensation stated.

**Razorpay, Forward Deployed Engineer, Bengaluru.** SDE2 or above, four or more years, with exceptional less-experienced candidates considered. The seat is embedded with one large merchant, described as owning "one merchant's technical destiny" end to end, as the single engineering point of contact with no handoffs. Requires Python plus one of Java, Go or TypeScript; APIs, webhooks, cloud on AWS or GCP, distributed-systems debugging; demonstrated end-to-end ownership of shipped products with customer contact; and, notably, "daily fluency with AI coding tools and agents". It commits to acknowledging issues within two hours. No compensation stated.

**Peakflo, Forward Deployed Engineer, India remote.** Listed via Y Combinator. Zero-point-five to two years of experience, voice and agentic workflows for finance. Stated range ₹10 to 13 lakh. This is the only India-located posting in the whole set that states a number.

**SigNoz, Forward Deployed Engineer, remote, India time zones.** Verified as a listing.

**HackerRank, Forward Deployed Engineer, Bengaluru, hybrid.** Four to seven years. A "full-stack builder comfortable working with web applications, IDEs, APIs, back-end services"; "confident speaking onsite with enterprise customers"; a clear communicator who can "go deep with engineers or simplify for non-technical crowds". Marked as no longer accepting applications at the time it was read. No compensation stated.

**Scale AI, Forward Deployed Engineer, India.** Two years preferred, remote initially, based in or willing to relocate to Mumbai or Bengaluru. Closed, removed in July 2025.

**C3.ai, Bengaluru.** Listed on aggregators, with a search excerpt indicating two or more years and six or more for the senior variant. The aggregator page itself returned an error, so treat this one as unverified and probably stale.

**Anthropic.** Appointed a Managing Director for India in January 2026, ahead of opening a Bengaluru office. That is confirmed from Anthropic's own announcement. Its FDE posting, fetched directly from its applicant-tracking system, lists only New York, San Francisco and Seattle.

## What the verified list tells you

Four things, and they are more useful than any salary chart.

**Experience floors match the US.** Two to five at Sarvam, four or more at Razorpay, four to seven at HackerRank. India is not an easier door on experience. The only genuinely junior India-located posting in the set is Peakflo at half a year to two years.

**None of them state compensation.** Not one of the six India-located postings gives a range. Peakflo, which does, is a YC listing rather than a company careers page. This is a structural difference from the US market, where pay-transparency law forces a number onto many postings, and it means you will negotiate without an anchor. Phase 08 covers what to do about that.

**India roles are as agent-heavy as any US role.** Sarvam names MCP servers, LangGraph, multi-agent orchestration and evaluation pipelines. Razorpay requires daily fluency with AI coding tools and agents. If you were expecting the India market to want less, it does not.

**Remote-from-India at the frontier labs is not visible in any posting.** This is the important negative result, and it is the next section.

## Part two: claims that appear only on marketing sites

The following circulate widely and could not be traced to a fetchable posting or a primary source.

**"The frontier labs hire FDEs remote from India."** This appears on India-focused content-marketing sites. It does not appear in any posting that could be opened. Every lab posting that was verified is US-office based, hybrid, with 25% to 50% travel. Anthropic's India MD appointment is real; an Anthropic India FDE posting is not confirmed.

**Detailed India salary bands.** One site publishes bands from ₹18 to 28 lakh for junior startup roles up to ₹90 lakh to ₹1.5 crore for senior roles at global labs. Another cites a Glassdoor Bengaluru average of ₹17 lakh with a 25th-to-75th percentile of ₹11.1 to 36.2 lakh, and quotes a staffing-industry figure of around ₹40 lakh for experienced FDEs. These are aggregator and survey figures published by companies that sell into this audience. The second of those two sites states the correct caveat itself: "The salary figure is meaningless without employer tier context."

**City distribution and headcount plans.** Bengaluru around 45% of listings, Hyderabad around 22%, NCR around 15%; named IT-services firms "scaling toward 100 FDEs" each. Single-source, own-data, no methodology published.

**"Most roles are filled through referrals rather than public boards."** Repeated on the same sites. It is plausible, it matches how mid-senior engineering hiring generally works in India, and it has no cited evidence. If you act on it, act on it because referral-first hiring is the safe assumption everywhere, not because this claim is established.

**The one directional anchor worth keeping.** A vendor compensation report states that "international bands run 50 to 70% of US". It is a synthesis of secondary sources by a company selling to FDE teams. It is the only anchor available for what remote-from-India compensation might look like, and it should be treated as an order of magnitude and nothing more.

## Part three: the four routes that are actually verifiable

Given the evidence, four routes exist. Pick a primary and a backup.

**Route 1: India-HQ agentic startups.** Sarvam is the verified example, Peakflo is the junior-friendly one. What they want is exactly what Phase 04 and Phase 09 of this path build: shipped agents, an evaluation pipeline, MCP or tool-calling work, and evidence you ship rather than prototype. This is the highest-fit route for someone finishing this path, and the hardest thing about it is that these companies are few.

**Route 2: platform vendors with India delivery organisations.** Databricks runs a remote-India FDE role. This route rewards depth in one vendor stack and a certification, and it comes with travel to customers on a stated cadence. It is the most predictable route and the least glamorous.

**Route 3: India-timezone or India-remote product companies.** SigNoz is the verified example. Developer-tool and infrastructure companies hiring in Indian time zones. Less competition, real engineering, and the customer is usually another engineer, which changes the shape of the customer work considerably.

**Route 4: US-timezone remote.** Postings like Speedy Labs on Y Combinator's board that say "Remote, US time zones". This means night-shift overlap. It is a real route and people take it, and you should be honest with yourself about doing it for a year or more.

One route deliberately not on this list: **relocating for the job.** It can work, and it comes with a documented failure mode. An FDE candidate's first-person account of an ElevenLabs loop describes reaching a verbal yes and then having the offer rescinded because "they decided they only want to hire in SF" and "they saw some immigration risks". The candidate's own lesson is the right one to take: force clarity on location and visa status early, in the first or second conversation, rather than after five rounds.

## The Indian enterprise context is an advantage

One thing the marketing sites underplay because it is not a salary number.

The FDE skill set is about deploying inside somebody else's messy institution. India has an unusually rich supply of exactly those institutions, and being able to work in them is a real and rare capability. A co-operative bank running a decade-old core banking system with a KYC backlog. A hospital chain where discharge summaries are scanned PDFs. A textile exporter whose entire order book lives in one person's Excel file. A state department whose grievance portal must run on-prem because the data cannot leave the building.

Add the domestic specifics: UPI and the payments stack, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and its consent and localisation obligations, RBI guidance on where regulated data may sit, WhatsApp as the default interface for users who will never open a web app, and rooms where the working language is Hinglish and the executive summary must be in English.

None of that is a disadvantage in this role. It is domain fluency that a candidate flown in from elsewhere does not have, and it is directly usable in the bootcamps in Phase 09, three of which are Indian institutions on purpose.

## Do this now

Write two lines in your journal.

Line one: your primary route, and the single requirement you are furthest from today. If your primary is Route 1, that requirement is almost certainly "shipped production systems" rather than anything about agents.

Line two: your backup route, and the same. Then set a reminder for week 20 to re-read both lines, because by then you will know something you do not know now, which is whether you actually enjoy the customer half of the work.
