Flat vector illustration, editorial technical style, on a plain off-white paper
background (#FBFAF8). Filled shapes in deep ink indigo (#3B54A3) with a single
warm brass accent (#A8741C). Absolutely no other colours.

Hard constraints:
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no captions anywhere in the image.
- No people, no faces, no hands, no figures.
- No gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture, no glow, no photorealism.
- Strictly flat and face-on. No perspective, no isometric or axonometric
  projection, no stacked depth, no side faces on shapes, no drop shadows.
  Every shape is drawn flat as if seen from directly in front.
- Do not use black or dark grey anywhere. The only shape colours permitted are
  indigo (#3B54A3) and brass (#A8741C). Shadows and depth-shading get flattened
  to black in post-processing and ruin the image.
- Light background with dark shapes drawn on it. Do not fill the background
  dark.
- Generous empty margin around the subject; the shapes should occupy roughly the
  middle two thirds of the frame.
- Simple geometric forms only. Nothing intricate or ornamental.
- Square composition, 1024 x 1024.
