Start with the honest version, because most of what circulates about FDE hiring in India is written by content-marketing sites selling a course or a placement service, not by anyone who has fetched an actual posting. This page separates what is verified from what is not, and gives you the routes that are real.

## What is actually verified

A small number of India-located or India-remote FDE postings can be traced to a live, fetchable page:

- **Databricks, "AI Engineer, FDE," Remote-India.** Fully remote, with travel to customers roughly every four to eight weeks. The posting names GenAI, RAG, and agents alongside classical ML and cloud deployment, and asks for a graduate degree or equivalent.
- **Peakflo (a YC-backed company), Forward Deployed Engineer, India/Remote.** Stated range ₹10-13 lakh, 0.5-2 years of experience, focused on voice and agentic workflows for finance.
- **SigNoz, Forward Deployed Engineer, Remote, India time zones.**
- A handful of others, including Hired's remote India posting and HackerRank's Bengaluru postings on Greenhouse, exist but are thinner on detail or have shown signs of closing.
- **Sarvam (Bengaluru)** and **Razorpay (Bengaluru)** run India-based FDE-shaped postings, and by the research behind this path, they are as agent-heavy in their requirements as any comparable US posting: Sarvam names MCP and LangGraph experience and asks for two to five years; Razorpay asks for four or more years and "daily fluency with AI coding tools and agents."

Glassdoor India lists roughly 373 postings matching "forward deployed engineer," and Indeed India roughly 1,000; both figures are keyword matches, not a count of verified real FDE roles, and should be treated as an upper bound on noise rather than a count of opportunities.

## What is not verified: remote-from-India at a frontier lab

Anthropic appointed Irina Ghose as managing director for India in January 2026, ahead of opening a Bengaluru office, which is real and confirmed. Search results also surface claims that Anthropic has an FDE posting specifically for Bengaluru. That could not be confirmed from Anthropic's own careers page: the live posting fetched for this research lists only New York, San Francisco, and Seattle, with no India location.

More broadly, the claim that OpenAI, Anthropic, or Palantir hire FDEs to work remote from India appears only on India-focused content-marketing sites, fde.academy, and similar guides selling courses or placement content, and does not appear in any posting that could actually be fetched and read. Every frontier-lab posting verified for this path is a US office location with stated travel, typically twenty-five to fifty percent, and a hybrid office expectation. That does not mean it never happens; individual exceptions and relocations exist that would not show up as a standing job posting. It means you should not plan your path around an unverified claim, and you should treat anyone selling you that promise with real skepticism.

## The four routes that are actually real

**1. Platform vendors with India delivery teams.** Databricks is the clearest verified example; Google Cloud, Salesforce, and ServiceNow run similar patterns according to secondary guides, though not all were independently verified for this path. These roles are the closest thing to a large, established company's engineering practices, with an India-based team and periodic, not constant, travel.

**2. India-HQ or India-timezone startups.** Sarvam, Razorpay, and SigNoz are the verified examples. These roles skew toward the applied-AI archetype covered earlier in this path: agents, evals, RAG, MCP, over deep infrastructure work.

**3. Remote-first companies in the Deel or Rippling mould**, where the company's whole operating model assumes a distributed team rather than treating India as a satellite office. These are less common in the FDE-specific postings verified for this research, but the pattern exists in the broader remote-work market.

**4. US startups posting "Remote, US time zones."** A verified example is a YC company's listing for a Forward Deployed Engineer, Remote, US time zones. Read this route honestly: it means real-time overlap with a US customer during India night hours, not a flexible remote arrangement. This is a genuine route into the role, and it is also the route with the highest personal cost, and worth weighing against the other three before you commit to chasing it.

## Comp: state plainly what is missing

Not one verified India posting in this research states a compensation figure, apart from Peakflo's stated ₹10-13 lakh. Databricks India, Sarvam, Razorpay, and HackerRank all omit comp from their postings entirely. Local-market guides, which are secondary sources built from their own aggregated data rather than fetched postings, put junior startup FDE roles around ₹18-28 lakh per annum and senior roles at global labs' India offices as high as ₹90 lakh to ₹1.5 crore, with one recruiter quoted at roughly ₹40 lakh for an experienced FDE and a Glassdoor Bengaluru average around ₹17 lakh. Treat every one of those figures as directional, not as a number to negotiate against directly; the next lesson in this module covers how to negotiate when the posting itself states nothing.

## Why most of this fills through referral

The same local-market guides state, consistently, that most India FDE roles fill through referral rather than public job boards. This is not a reason to stop watching the boards; it is a reason not to rely on them alone. Concretely: the highest-leverage use of your time in this path is not refreshing India job boards. It is building the proof-of-work this module covers, and then using it to get warm introductions into the companies on the list above, through people who already work there, through the a16z-style founder and FDE networks mentioned earlier in this path, or through direct, specific outreach that names a real gap in their product you have already thought about.

One more honest note on travel and language: local guides describe four to ten days a month of customer-site travel across India and the wider Asia-Pacific region for India-based platform-vendor roles, and note that Hinglish business communication is often expected for BFSI and government customers specifically. Neither of those is a deal-breaker; both are worth knowing before you accept an offer rather than after.
