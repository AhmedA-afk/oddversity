Oddversity is for the person who can make a chatbot demo but wants to understand what happens when the demo meets real data, tools, permissions, and a budget.

## A path instead of a pile

AI education often presents a catalog of disconnected topics. Oddversity starts with foundations, then moves through prompting, context, retrieval, tool use, agents, evaluation, and production. The point is not that everyone needs every lesson. The point is that the dependencies are visible.

The [curriculum map](/learn) is the main product. Interview questions, quizzes, and scenarios are practice layers around it: ways to retrieve the ideas after the first read and apply them to a decision.

## Free changes the feedback loop

There is no locked lesson here. That makes it easier to see where the sequence is unclear, where an example is missing, and which explanations are useful outside the author’s head. The trade-off is that distribution and maintenance matter more than a paywall.

## What we will measure

Not time-on-page. A better signal is whether readers can move from a mental model to a small, tested implementation: a retrieved answer with provenance, a tool call with validation, or an eval that changes a release decision.

The work is still early. The standard is simple: clear explanations, honest limits, and fewer mysterious boxes in the systems people ship.
